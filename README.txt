PROJECT STRUCTURE:
Please start by opening "index.html". The pages are organized in the
following order, matching the numbered buttons on the website:

open "index.html" in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox
or Safari)

START HERE:
1. index.html -> Dashboard
   - The primary landing page featuring national waste statistics,
     key charts, and project overview.

2. report.html -> About the report
   - The 7-part project report covering problem definitions, economic
     impacts, environmental and health concerns, source segregation,
     the proposed solution, economic feasibility, and baseline
     validation with limitations and next steps.

3. Waste2Value_ how a one-tonne-a-day recovery business works.html
   -> The Business Model
   - Core operational blueprint detailing source segregation, 5 revenue
     streams, cost structures, risk sensitivity, and pilot phases.

4. calculator.html -> Surplus calculator
   - Interactive financial sensitivity model to test monthly surplus
     and break-even points by adjusting household subscriptions,
     recyclable prices, and wages.

5. https://www.instagram.com/waste2value.in/ -> Instagram
   - Project social presence for outreach and initiative updates.



- Live Netlify URL (Cloud Backup): https://waste-2-value.netlify.app


NOTES:
- National statistics on the dashboard show their source under each
  figure.
- The financial figures on the business model page and in the
  calculator are illustrative planning assumptions, not market
  quotations. The first three months of the pilot would replace them
  with local data (surveys, rate cards, quotations and a waste audit).
