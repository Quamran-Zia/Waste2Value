What stays fixed

  • Institutional and commercial contracts: ₹40,000 a month
  • Compost sales: ₹12,000 a month
  • Training and audits: ₹10,000 a month
  • All running costs except wages: ₹67,000 a month

Household income is ₹80 per household a month, and recyclables are about 10 tonnes a month, which is about 38% of the 26 tonnes collected. If the real share were 20% to 25% (5.2 to 6.5 tonnes), revenue would be ₹28,000 to ₹38,000 lower. For the wage slider: five people at about ₹15,000 cost ₹75,000, five people at ₹18,000 to ₹19,000 cost ₹90,000 to ₹95,000, and a team that has to grow to eight to ten people at ₹15,000 costs ₹1.2 to ₹1.5 lakh. The full breakdown is on the business model page.

All figures are illustrative planning assumptions, not market quotations. Start-up capital is not included. Replace them with local quotations before any real launch.
